TAYF is a small, quiet puzzle game about light.

Every level gives you a handful of optical pieces: prisms that split white
light into red, green and blue, mirrors that bounce a beam, and lenses that
focus it. Drag a piece onto the board, rotate it with your finger or the fine
tuning pad, and route the beams into every target before the timer runs out.

FEATURES

- Six hand-made levels, then an endless ladder of generated puzzles
- A new daily puzzle, the same for everyone
- Par scores, medals, coins, hints and a small shop
- Generous touch controls: drag, rotate, nudge and remove, plus 0.1 degree
  fine tuning for the pieces that need it
- All sound is synthesised on the device; there are no audio files
- Progress is stored locally on the phone
- Works fully offline. No ads, no analytics, no network permission.
